/goal
Make immutable domain updates convenient in FunnySharp without making immutable storage or copying the default cost of every operation. Completion means the library interoperates cleanly with records, System.Collections.Immutable, FrozenDictionary, FrozenSet, and other relevant BCL types, and offers a deliberately small optics/update surface for clear, composable nested reads and updates where ordinary with-expressions become noisy. Identity, composition, missing-focus, mutation, aliasing, and allocation behavior must be documented and tested; APIs must not label mutable views as immutable or expose unsafe lifetimes. The scope excludes a new persistent-collection ecosystem and a broad category-theory optics hierarchy; any Lens, Optional, or Traversal-style abstraction that remains must justify itself through ordinary C# use cases and predictable inference. Evidence must include xUnit v3 law/edge-case tests, record and BCL-collection examples, and allocation comparisons with direct with-expressions or collection builders.
