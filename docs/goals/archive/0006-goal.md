/goal
Give FunnySharp a discoverable data-pipeline experience inspired by tidyverse and Clojure while remaining native to the .NET ecosystem. Completion means the supported transformation verbs are consistently named and shaped, compose left-to-right, and work directly with the appropriate BCL primitives such as IEnumerable<T>, IAsyncEnumerable<T>, ReadOnlySpan<T>, Span<T>, ReadOnlyMemory<T>, and Memory<T> without pretending that all of them share identical lifetime or laziness semantics. Pipelines must avoid hidden repeated enumeration and unnecessary intermediate collections; hot paths must have zero-copy or fused alternatives where the underlying data and safety rules permit them. Cancellation, disposal, ordering, streaming, and deferred-execution behavior must be explicit. The library must not create its own general collection ecosystem or make eager materialization the default. Evidence must include xUnit v3 semantic tests, examples of realistic data cleaning/transformation flows, and benchmarks against the closest idiomatic LINQ, loop, Span, or async-stream baseline.
