/goal
Establish whether a bounded, explicit subset of FunnySharp's composable calculations can be translated into specialized C# execution that preserves their semantics and materially improves the trade-off between consumer expression and runtime cost. Completion is a maintainer-ready adopt, adapt, defer or reject decision backed by an executable representative decoder or validation calculation and a combined filtering, mapping or folding workload; prose-only feasibility claims do not suffice. Compare the proposed generated path with its ordinary delegated implementation, appropriate current LINQ or specialist alternatives and a fair hand-written loop, accounting for generation and build cost, diagnostics, debugging, code size, allocations, copying and execution performance on the disclosed environment. Establish exact supported constructs and an honest treatment of unsupported or dynamic composition. Callback count and order, exceptions, short-circuiting, cancellation where relevant, destination capacity and partial writes must match the promised semantics; purity, associativity and safe reordering cannot be inferred from an arbitrary delegate. C# source generation must remain additive and opt-in rather than pretending to rewrite arbitrary existing code, and unsupported behavior must not silently fall back with different guarantees. Keep Span and asynchronous lifetimes truthful and assess actual trimming or AOT evidence only for the supported forms. The research may include a narrow prototype, but cannot silently commit a universal compiler, custom language, new scheduler or wholesale LINQ replacement. Use Goals 0026-0027 or 0034 as representative calculations where available without requiring their complete future breadth. A supported negative finding completes this research outcome and does not claim the optimization feature has shipped.
