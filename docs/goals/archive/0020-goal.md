/goal
Establish credible performance trust for FunnySharp so that choosing its normal stable APIs does not impose an undisclosed performance burden. Completion means every performance-relevant stable operation has documented algorithmic complexity, enumeration, allocation, boxing, materialization, buffering, and async scheduling characteristics, and the repository has reproducible semantically equivalent comparisons against the appropriate raw BCL primitive or loop, idiomatic high-level BCL or LINQ implementation, FSharp.Core where the representation and semantics are comparable, and relevant Funcky, CSharpFunctionalExtensions, or language-ext operations. No stable default path may retain a known algorithmic cliff, unbounded resource behavior, hidden repeated work, or material regression that is neither removed nor justified by additional visible semantics; the existing long-chain StateTransition composition issue must therefore be resolved or excluded from the stable contract. APIs positioned as faster alternatives must statistically tie or beat the closest high-level baseline on a controlled environment, while raw handwritten BCL remains an honest reference rather than a benchmark selected to manufacture a win. Throughput regressions become blocking only after hardware, runtime, noise floor, and comparison policy are controlled; allocation and complexity regressions may be blocked independently. Evidence includes source-bound benchmark definitions and results, environment metadata, equivalence justifications, regression policy, and generated public performance guidance. Unsupported blanket claims such as zero cost, always faster, or faster IO are prohibited.
