/goal
Make pure state transitions and finite-state workflows substantially easier to express, test, and review in FunnySharp. Completion means users can model composable state transformations and state machines in which valid transitions, invalid events, produced outputs or commands, and transition failures are explicit; the transition core can be deterministic and tested without executing effects, while asynchronous work and cancellation remain outside or at a clearly visible boundary. Undefined transitions must be detectable rather than silently ignored, and histories can be replayed to reproduce a state when callers retain the events. The feature must remain a library abstraction rather than becoming a workflow engine, actor runtime, persistence system, or distributed orchestrator, and it must not depend on a general discriminated-union implementation. Evidence must include xUnit v3 tests for transition coverage, determinism, replay, invalid inputs, and sync/async boundaries, plus at least one realistic state-machine example that is clearer than an equivalent nested conditional implementation.
