/goal
Curate FunnySharp's advanced functional patterns according to the maintainer-accepted decisions from Goal 1 so that every pattern exposed as stable earns its place in ordinary modern C# code. Completion means the existing effect and resource boundary, pure state-transition and commands-as-data model, optional immutable-update or optics surface, and every additional advanced capability in the decision record are aligned with that record: keep, adopt, and adapt decisions are delivered as small BCL-native stable abstractions with compelling normal-application use cases; experimental decisions are unmistakably experimental; deferred capabilities are absent from the stable surface; and remove decisions are removed. A retained abstraction must demonstrably reduce consumer ceremony or prevent a meaningful bug, compose with the semantic core and async model, preserve clear evaluation and resource-lifetime semantics, and have acceptable measured cost. State decisions must remain deterministic and separable from command execution; resource scopes must be safe across success, failure, exception, and cancellation; immutable programming must remain opt-in; and environment-dependent effects must not become a DI container or execution universe. Rejected or deferred capabilities must not be smuggled back through aliases or convenience APIs. Evidence includes the accepted decision references, stable-versus-experimental API inventory, realistic comparative examples, xUnit v3 semantic tests, and relevant allocation and throughput results. Completion does not mean reproducing language-ext's abstraction hierarchy or feature count.
