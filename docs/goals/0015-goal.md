/goal
Bring FunnySharp's business-outcome core to a coherent, friendly, and rigorous completion state around exactly four canonical semantic carriers: Option<T> for absence, Result<TValue, TError> for fail-fast value-producing work, UnitResult<TError> for success-or-failure work with no meaningful value, and Validation<TValue, TError> for deterministic accumulation of independent errors. Completion means ordinary business workflows can construct, inspect, match, map, bind where semantically valid, validate, recover, combine, and cross the synchronous/asynchronous boundary without dummy values, nested tuple ceremony, unsafe payload access, ambiguous null behavior, or implicit conversions that conceal which branch was created. Maybe<T> must not exist as a second absence carrier. Default and uninitialized Result, UnitResult, and Validation values must not masquerade as legitimate domain outcomes; Option's default remains a valid None. Absence, expected failure, accumulated validation errors, exceptions, and cancellation remain distinct channels, and Validation must not acquire a Bind contract that falsely promises error accumulation for dependent computations. Equality, hashing, nullability, nesting, exception preservation, cancellation, Task, and ValueTask behavior must be explicit. Evidence includes public-API inventory, compiling business examples, xUnit v3 semantic and law tests, misuse and edge-case tests, and call-site comparisons with straightforward idiomatic C#. A general union framework and competitor compatibility APIs are out of scope.
