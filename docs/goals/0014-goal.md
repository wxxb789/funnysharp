/goal
Establish an authoritative next-stage product constitution and capability decision record for FunnySharp. Completion means the current FunnySharp public surface and the relevant feature families in the then-current .NET/BCL, FSharp.Core, Funcky, CSharpFunctionalExtensions, and language-ext baselines have been examined without silent omissions, and each FunnySharp API has a justified keep, redesign, experimental, or remove decision while each relevant external capability has an adopt, adapt, defer, or reject decision. Decisions must be based on ordinary C# usefulness, reduction of consumer-side semantic LOC, clarity from signatures, compile-time verifiability, AI predictability, BCL interoperability, async and cancellation semantics, and credible performance feasibility. The result must define one canonical vocabulary and stability boundary accepted by the repository maintainer; unresolved qualitative judgments remain explicitly unverified rather than being guessed. FunnySharp remains independent: no competitor dependency, carrier conversion, compatibility package, naming concession, or migration promise is in scope. The contract must continue to reject a custom runtime, scheduler, replacement collection universe, pervasive immutability, HKT or monad-transformer hierarchy, and a premature general discriminated-union system. Evidence consists of version- or commit-pinned inventories, representative side-by-side call sites, recorded rationales, and a complete decision matrix. This is a product and scope decision goal, not an instruction to implement every surveyed feature.
