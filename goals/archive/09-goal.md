/goal
Make common concurrent functional workflows safe and explicit in FunnySharp using .NET's Task, ValueTask, IAsyncEnumerable<T>, CancellationToken, Channel, and related BCL primitives rather than a competing concurrency runtime. Completion means users can express bounded parallel mapping/traversal, controlled racing or first-success behavior, and deterministic result aggregation with documented ordering, failure, cancellation, and cleanup semantics. Operations must not leak orphaned work, lose exceptions, block synchronously on asynchronous work, accidentally consume ValueTask more than once, or ignore backpressure where streaming is promised. Fail-fast and error-accumulating variants must be distinguishable rather than controlled by surprising defaults. Evidence must include deterministic xUnit v3 tests for success, partial failure, simultaneous failure, cancellation, timeout, disposal, concurrency limits, and ordering, plus throughput/allocation measurements against the closest BCL implementation.
