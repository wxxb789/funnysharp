/goal
Establish FunnySharp as a clean, buildable, testable, and packageable functional-programming library baseline for .NET 10 and later. Completion means a clean checkout can restore, build, run an xUnit v3 test suite, and produce valid packages; the repository records an authoritative product contract that keeps the library pragmatic, BCL-first, performance-conscious, idiomatic to C#, and consistent across synchronous and asynchronous APIs where both are meaningful. The core must not depend on ASP.NET Core, a custom runtime, or a large functional abstraction stack. Immutable data must remain opt-in rather than the default. General discriminated unions and analyzers are explicitly out of scope until revisited after the official C# 15/.NET 11 union design stabilizes. Evidence must include the clean build/test/package results, the public package/dependency boundaries, and a concise statement of these accepted constraints that later goals can be audited against. Do not add speculative feature APIs merely to make the foundation appear complete.
