/goal
Independently verify whether FunnySharp satisfies every accepted completion contract from Goals 1–12 and issue an evidence-backed final verdict without treating repository claims or a green test suite as sufficient proof. The audit must evaluate the actual public API and behavior for the .NET 10+ foundation; BCL-first and performance-first constraints; sync/async consistency; function composition, currying, and partial application; Option; Result and fail-fast railway flows; accumulating Validation and traversal; tidy and zero-copy-friendly pipelines; state/state-machine semantics; thin effects and resource safety; structured concurrency; opt-in immutability and pragmatic optics; ASP.NET Core integration; documentation, packaging, trimming/Native AOT claims, and compatibility. It must also confirm the agreed exclusions: no custom runtime, no replacement collection ecosystem, no default immutable-data tax, no large typeclass/monad-transformer hierarchy, no analyzer requirement, and no premature general discriminated-union implementation before the official C# 15/.NET 11 design is stable. Completion evidence is a goal-by-goal pass/fail matrix that links each verdict to reproducible build, xUnit v3, integration, documentation-compilation, API-inventory, dependency, and benchmark evidence; records every material gap with a minimal reproduction or precise missing artifact; distinguishes correctness defects from design or evidence gaps; and gives an unambiguous overall acceptance verdict. If any material criterion is unverified or failed, the overall result must not be reported as complete. This goal is an audit and evaluation boundary, not an instruction to conceal failures by implementing a new scope during verification.
