/goal
Make absence explicit and safe in FunnySharp through a focused Option abstraction and idiomatic bridges to existing .NET absence conventions. Completion means users can construct, inspect, match, transform, bind, filter, combine, and provide fallbacks for optional values without unsafe value access or ambiguous null semantics; interoperation with nullable references, Nullable<T>, Try-pattern APIs, collection lookup, and synchronous/asynchronous computations is documented and predictable. The abstraction must have well-defined equality, hashing, default-value, nesting, and null policies, must not silently catch arbitrary exceptions, and must not introduce a general-purpose discriminated-union system. Evidence must include xUnit v3 tests for laws and all representation edge cases, compiling examples for BCL interop, and measurements showing the cost of common operations and conversions.
